FAQ

What kind of projects do you take on?

Most of my work falls into three buckets: cleaning up messy data so it's actually usable, building dashboards and reports that answer a specific business question, and automating the manual spreadsheet work that eats up a team's week. If you're not sure your project fits, ask below — happy to take a look.

Which tools do you work with?

Day to day, mostly Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI. I pick the tool based on the problem rather than forcing everything through one system — a quick answer might live in a spreadsheet, while a recurring report is usually worth automating.

How do you handle sensitive or confidential data?

I'm happy to sign an NDA before any data changes hands, and I work with anonymized or sample data wherever that's practical. Raw client data stays out of anything public, including the examples shown here.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused dashboard or cleanup usually takes one to two weeks; larger reporting builds or ongoing analysis can run longer. I'll give you a real estimate once I understand the scope — it's usually one of the first things we sort out.

How do we get started?

Send a message below with a bit about what you're trying to solve, or reach out directly through the links in the sidebar. I typically reply within a business day.

Have more questions?

Hi. Ask me anything, and I'll answer right away.
